Indiana proved it's possible

Indiana University was ranked 72nd in talent — yet they won the College Football Playoff. They found the players others missed.

The problem? Recruiting sites like 247 Sports rely on high school ratings that don't get updated after players develop. Coaches at smaller programs are left digging through stale data, struggling to fill crucial team gaps with overlooked talent.

Portal Pair surfaces players beyond the rankings — with opponent-adjusted stats, transfer portal visibility, and a single score that shows real impact. Give every program a chance to find their star.
